I am a Visiting Assistant Professor in Statistics at [Gustavus Adolphus College](https://gustavus.edu/) and a statistician and data scientist with experience in financial services. I teach statistics and data analysis and work across statistical inference, predictive modeling, and machine learning.

I earned my PhD in [Statistics](https://cas.okstate.edu/statistics/) at Oklahoma State University in July 2026, working under [Professor Pratyaydipta Rudra](https://experts.okstate.edu/prudra) on evaluating the utility of normality tests in statistical practice. I hold an MS in [Mathematics](https://academics.ysu.edu/mathematics-and-statistics) from Youngstown State University and a BS in Actuarial Science from [Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology](https://www.knust.edu.gh/).

I hail from Lawra-Tolibri in the Upper West Region of Ghana and am a proud Xavascan. Outside academia, I co-founded Konbar, a social enterprise tackling malnutrition and food insecurity through sustainable dry-season irrigation farming in rural Africa. I enjoy teaching, tutoring statistics, serving as a science fair judge, listening to music, and meaningful conversations. Go Pokes!

Read more about this community work in [Study International](https://studyinternational.com/news/nobel-peace-prize/) and [YFMJ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xGNqkI32yX0).
